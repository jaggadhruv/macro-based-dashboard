Technical buy setups

Names whose chart structure, base, volatility and volume all agree, with a defined entry, stop and target. Every setup must clear an eight-point trend filter, keep the stop within 12% of entry, and offer at least 2:1 reward to risk — otherwise it is not listed. Ordered by quality. Click a column to sort.

These are entry mechanics, not predictions. A setup like this might work 40–50% of the time; it makes money only because the winners run further than the losers, and that arithmetic collapses if the stop is not taken. On synthetic data this filter fires on roughly a third of genuine base-and-uptrend charts, about 6% of pure random walks and 1% of downtrends — patterns do appear in noise, so the sector zone above is the filter that matters most.
